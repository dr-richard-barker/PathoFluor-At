listed by title and date only. Drive links are private and are not published. Nothing here has
been copied into the repo unless the "In repo" column says so.

## Related but not part of this study

- **Water-stress / flood reflectance work (2015–2019):** "Report_Hyper spectral imaging_March_2015",
  "Arabidopsis Hyper spectral water stress" (2018/2022), "Hyper spectral point measurements",
  "Representative images for water stress point measurements", "Hyperspec_plant_data"

  WARF poster (Gao, Singh, Barker, Nimunkar, Couture, Kingdon, Gilroy), "intro's for WARF hyper &
  LightBox" (2017), "Automation of hyperspectral imaging" (2018), HyperScanner CNC photos/notes
  (2019–2020), "Picasso HyperScanner proposal" (2019), "HyperScanner_storyboard" (2025), and a
  2019 *Plant Methods* paper PDF ("Lien, Baker, et al. 2019", s13007-019-0392-1). This is the
  follow-on instrument that the 2016 protocol's Goal 2 anticipated. It can be cited in the
  Discussion once its reference is checked against Crossref.
